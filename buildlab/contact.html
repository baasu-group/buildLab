Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can BuildLab discuss a software project?

Yes. Send us your goals and constraints. We will confirm the scope, relevant contributors, and availability before proposing work.

How does collaboration work?

We understand the problem, plan and design the work, build in shared iterations, then review, test, deploy, and improve it.

How can I join the team?

Use the Join BuildLab email link to introduce yourself, your role, and examples of your work. Team participation depends on current needs and review.

Is the six-month internship still available?

The mentorship program and its learning tracks remain part of BuildLab. The application form is on the Mentorship page.
