# BuildLab content administration

Keep project and team claims factual and approved before publishing. Do not add private organizational details to public website files.

## Team profiles

Edit `team.js` to add a profile. A profile renders only when both `visible: true` and `roleVerified: true` are set. Confirm the person's current role, preferred public name, profile link, and consent before enabling visibility. Remove or set `visible: false` to unpublish. Do not include private contact details.

## Projects and expertise

The homepage currently shows the learning resource hub as ongoing internal work and the Team Management Platform as planned internal work. Add completed or client projects only after confirming the status, technologies, BuildLab contribution, public link, and permission to name any client. The four published expertise areas correspond to documented tracks. Confirm qualified contributors before adding Mobile Development, DevOps, SaaS, or System Architecture as offered capabilities.

## Contact and mentorship

Project and participation inquiries use `buildlab.np@gmail.com`. The existing six-month mentorship form continues to use FormSubmit. Check that the form service and mailbox remain active before a public launch. Learning tracks and resources are maintained in the existing `INT-026-08-01` folders and `resources/data.js`.
