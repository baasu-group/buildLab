# BuildLab content administration

Keep project and team claims factual and approved before publishing. Do not add private organizational details to public website files.

## Team profiles

Edit `team.js` to add a profile. A profile renders only when both `visible: true` and `roleVerified: true` are set. Confirm the person's current role, preferred public name, profile link, and consent before enabling visibility. Remove or set `visible: false` to unpublish. Do not include private contact details.

## Projects and expertise

Add completed or client projects only after confirming the status, technologies, BuildLab contribution, public link, and permission to name any client. The internship program has four tracks: UI/UX Design, Frontend, Backend, and Quality Assurance. The DevOps and AI/ML paths are supplementary learning resources, not separate internship tracks. The business-focused Expertise section is a different audience-facing overview. Confirm qualified contributors before adding Mobile Development, Cloud/DevOps, SaaS, or System Architecture as offered capabilities. The suggested BiiQuiz, Himalayan Peak & Trails, Dockly, and Sync Meetings case studies must stay unpublished until BuildLab’s role and publication rights are confirmed. Use `docs/CASE_STUDY_TEMPLATE.md` for approved project writeups.

## Contact and mentorship

Project and participation inquiries use `buildlab.np@gmail.com`. The existing six-month mentorship form continues to use FormSubmit. Check that the form service and mailbox remain active before a public launch. Learning tracks and resources are maintained in the existing `INT-026-08-01` folders and `resources/data.js`.

## Audience pages and Journal

The industry, emerging talent, and project partnership pages have separate inquiry forms. The Journal contains proposed topics only. Use `docs/EDITORIAL_WORKFLOW.md` before publishing an article. Keep the parent organization’s legal name private on public pages unless the owner changes that instruction.
