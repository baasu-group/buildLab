## Problem

Describe the user or business problem. Do not disclose confidential requirements.

## Solution

Explain what was built and which parts are live. Separate delivered work from planned work.

## Engineering approach

Name only the technologies and decisions confirmed by the people who worked on the project. Describe major tradeoffs without exposing private infrastructure or source code.

## Outcome

Use verified results or a factual description of the current state. Do not add invented metrics, testimonials, or customer associations.

## Team contribution

State exactly what BuildLab contributors did. Distinguish their work from work by another organization or a client.

## Review before publishing

- Confirm the status, links, technologies, and contributions with project leads.
- Obtain permission to name clients, partners, products, and contributors.
- Check screenshots and text for confidential information.
- Record the reviewer and approval date.
