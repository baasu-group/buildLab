Mentorship

Learning stays part of the work.

Experienced mentors support new contributors through a six-month internship program with guided practice, track-specific roadmaps, reviews, and collaborative project work.

The six-month journey

Three months to prepare. Three months to deliver.

The first phase builds strong foundations through learning and practice. The second phase applies those foundations to intense, real-world project work.

PHASE 01 · MONTHS 01–03

Learning & practice

Build the fundamentals, tools, habits, and confidence needed to contribute effectively.

MONTH 01

Orient & learn

Understand the product, users, roles, workflow, tools, and core concepts for your track.

Output: learning plan + product brief
MONTH 02

Practice the craft

Complete guided exercises, small assignments, reviews, and cross-track collaboration practice.

Output: reviewed practice work
MONTH 03

Build foundations

Apply your skills in a small integrated feature and prepare for production-level project work.

Output: foundation project + readiness review
PHASE 02 · MONTHS 04–06

Intensive real-world project

Work as a product team to design, build, test, review, and ship the Team Management Platform.

MONTH 04

Discover & plan

Refine requirements, user journeys, technical plans, architecture, backlog, and delivery goals.

Output: approved project plan
MONTH 05

Build & integrate

Implement the core product across UI/UX, frontend, backend, and QA with regular reviews.

Output: integrated product increment
MONTH 06

Test, ship & present

Stabilize the release, fix defects, document the work, demo the product, and complete the handoff.

Output: release + final presentation
